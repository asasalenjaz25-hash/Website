01 · Foundation

Where the colours come from

The logo is a single deep navy, #06245E, set in a wide, spaced sans-serif. The whole palette is built from that navy. Every blue shares its hue (220°), so the site reads as one brand instead of a template.

Direction: Trust & Authority

A technical-works contractor is chosen for reliability. Navy says competence and dependability. One warm gold accent adds the energy and visibility a service business needs for calls to action, and it echoes the yellow safety tones of the trade. This matches the "Authority navy + trust gold" profile recommended for professional services.

What this fixes

Today

The template mixes green, yellow, orange, red, bright blue and a purple, in a different combination on each home template. Buttons are orange on one page and blue on the next.

Proposed

Two brand families: navy/blue for structure and actions, and gold as the single accent. Neutrals are tinted towards the navy so greys never look muddy next to the logo.

06 · Implementation

How it will be applied

The template generates each colour's full 50–900 scale from three HSL values. Changing those values in one override stylesheet recolours the entire site, with no page-by-page edits.

Hard-coded coloursPurple #301996, orange #EB6029, several navies (#041B34, #0B163F, #0D1C5D…)Mapped to ASAS Navy, Deep Navy or Action Blue
FontsParkinsans (+4 others)Poppins headings, Open Sans body

Do

  • Use one gold button per screen, for the main action
  • Put navy text on gold, never white
  • Use Action Blue for everything clickable
  • Put a navy overlay on hero photos so white text stays readable
  • Pair colour with text or an icon for status

Don't

  • Bring back orange, green, red or purple accents
  • Use gold for body text or small text on white
  • Stretch, recolour or add effects to the logo
  • Put the navy logo on dark backgrounds (use the white one)
  • Use pure black (#000) text

Next step: on approval, the palette and fonts are applied through one override stylesheet and checked page by page (desktop and mobile) before hand-over.
