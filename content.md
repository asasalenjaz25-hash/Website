## Part 2 · Template research

### 2.1 What the template is

Repio is a multipurpose **home-services** template: cleaning, laundry, handyman, AC, plumbing, roofing and device repair. It ships:

- **11 home-page designs**, each themed for one consumer trade. Here they serve as `/services/*` pages.
- **19 inner pages**, including a full **e-commerce set** (shop, product, cart, checkout) and a **pricing** page.
- A consumer tone throughout: "Book Now", "$19 plans", "Trusted by 40+ clients", cleaning-product shop.

ASAS is a **B2B / high-value fit-out and MEP contractor**. Customers are developers, business owners, facility managers and villa owners who need a **quote and a site visit**, not a basket or a monthly plan. So the adaptation work is mostly *removing consumer features* and *rewriting* the page structures that already work.
